## Title Options
1. **Why I rejected the SaaS model for my dev tool (Perpetual License Case Study)**
2. **Building a "Local-First" business: $79 one-time vs $10/mo**

## Body Structure

### The Decision
I'm launching **CoDRAG** (a local code context engine).
Everyone told me to make it a subscription. "It's better for valuation." "MRR is king."

### The Reality
But as a developer, I *hate* subscriptions for tools that run on my own machine.
If I'm paying for the electricity and the CPU cycles, why am I paying you rent?

### The Model
So I went with **Pay Once, Own Forever** for the Pro version.
It aligns my incentives: I have to build a product good enough to buy, not just good enough to not cancel.

### Discussion
Am I crazy? Has anyone else here gone "Perpetual" in 2026?
