# Copy Preparation & Execution Hub

This directory contains the specific marketing copy, angles, and research notes for every prioritized channel.

## How to use this folder

1.  **Pick a Phase** from the `PRIORITY_CHANNELS.md` plan.
2.  **Open the corresponding Tier folder** below.
3.  **Read `angle.md`** to understand the strategy for that specific channel.
4.  **Open `post-draft.md`** to get the copy.
    *   *Note: These are drafts. You must customize the `[Links]` and review against current community rules before posting.*
5.  **Update `status.md`** to track your progress.

## Directory Structure

### 🟢 Tier 0: Must Activate (Foundation)
*High impact, perfect ICP fit. Do these first.*
- `r-LocalLLaMA` - The technical heart of local AI.
- `hacker-news-show-hn` - The big launch.
- `r-cursor` / `r-windsurf` - The direct user base.
- `blog-architecture-overview` - The canonical artifact everything links to.

### 🟡 Tier 1: Month 1-2 (Growth)
*Broad reach, specific niches.*
- `product-hunt` - The launch event.
- `r-programming` - Educational/discussion focus.
- `r-selfhosted` - Privacy/sovereignty focus.
- `r-MachineLearning` - Research/algorithm focus.
- `dev-to-series` - "Build in public" tutorials.

### 🔵 Tier 2: Month 2-3 (Sustain)
*Long-tail and specific ecosystems.*
- **IDE Communities:** `r-vscode`, `r-neovim`
- **Languages:** `r-rust`, `r-Python`
- **AI Ecosystem:** `r-ClaudeAI`, `r-OpenAI`
- **Platform/Infra:** `r-devops`, `r-kubernetes`
- **Tech Socials:** `lobsters`, `indie-hackers`, `linkedin`, `x-bluesky`

### ⚪ Tier 3: Outreach (SEO & Backlinks)
*Outbound pitching.*
- `awesome-lists` - PR templates.
- `newsletters` - Email pitches to editors.
- `podcasts` - Guest pitches to hosts.
- `discord-slack` - Community showcase messages.
- `medium-publications` - Syndication pitches.
