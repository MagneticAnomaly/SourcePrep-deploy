## Body Structure

### Intro: The "Context" Problem
We love AI coding tools. But we hated the "context lottery."
Sometimes RAG works. Sometimes it retrieves a unit test instead of the implementation.
When you're building production software, "sometimes" isn't good enough.

### The "Cloud" Problem
We also hated the binary choice:
1.  Use a dumb local tool.
2.  Upload your entire proprietary codebase to a startup's cloud to get "smart" features.

Why couldn't we have smarts *and* privacy?

### Building the Hard Way
We decided to build the hard thing: a local-first, graph-based indexer.
*   **Rust** for speed.
*   **Tree-sitter** for precision.
*   **MCP** for compatibility.

### The Business Decision
We also made a choice about how to sell it. No subscriptions. No "Usage Credits."
You buy the license, you run the software. It's yours.

### Join Us
This is the tool we wanted to use. We hope you like it too.

## Tone
Manifesto-style. Personal, principled, slightly defiant against the "SaaS/Cloud" status quo.
