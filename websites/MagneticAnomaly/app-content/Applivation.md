# Applivation
**Job applications made easy.**

## What is Applivation?
Applivation is an autofill for job applications. It maps your saved career history onto the grueling data entry required by ATS platforms — in seconds, not minutes — and syncs invisibly through iCloud across all your Apple devices. It never auto-submits. You review and confirm every field, so the candidate always holds the boarding pass.

## The Problem We're Solving
Applying for jobs online is repetitive, error-prone drudgery. Every applicant-tracking system asks for the same name, address, work history, and education — re-typed into a slightly different form, a hundred times over. The tedium wears people down long before they reach the roles they're genuinely excited about.

## How It Works
1. **Build your vault.** Save your career history, education, and links once. It lives in your personal iCloud — end-to-end encrypted, never on a third-party server.
2. **Open any application.** Applivation's Safari extension detects the form fields on the page, on-device.
3. **Review and confirm.** Applivation maps your vault to the form automatically. You verify the details and tap fill. It never submits for you.

## Why It's Different
- **Privacy-first, by architecture.** No third-party accounts, no subscriptions, zero data mining. Your details sync securely and invisibly through the iCloud you already trust.
- **One vault, everywhere you are.** iPhone, iPad, and Mac stay in lockstep. Start an application on your phone, finish it on your laptop.
- **You stay in control.** "Review and confirm" is the whole UX. Applivation fills — you decide whether it's right before anything goes anywhere.

## Availability
A universal purchase across iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with a Safari extension bundle for in-browser autofill. Live on the App Store at [applivation.app](https://applivation.app).
